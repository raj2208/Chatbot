# Elanco — Culture & Ways of Working

## What Elanco Culture Looks Like

Elanco describes its culture as **"startup speed, enterprise accountability."** The company grew quickly from a 30-person founding team to 3,200 people in 13 years, and there is a conscious effort to preserve the directness, ownership, and low-bureaucracy feel of its early days while building the governance structures that a company at this scale requires.

Three things employees consistently cite in engagement surveys:

1. **High ownership** — Individual contributors regularly make decisions that at larger companies would require multiple approval layers. A field sales rep can approve a clinic-level discount up to 5% without manager sign-off.
2. **Mission connection** — 84% of employees in the FY2024 engagement survey said they feel their work directly contributes to animal welfare. This number is unusually high for a company this size.
3. **Candour** — "Don't sugarcoat bad news" is a phrase attributed to CEO Arjun Dasgupta and has become part of the cultural vocabulary. Post-mortems are blameless and public within the team.

## Communication Norms

### Async-first, not async-only
- Slack is the primary communication tool for day-to-day work
- Default expectation: respond to Slack messages within 4 hours during core hours (10 AM–5 PM IST)
- Email is used for: external parties, formal approvals, and HR/compliance communications
- Do not ping someone's manager to chase a response — escalate directly after 24 hours

### Meeting Culture
- **No-meeting Wednesdays:** Wednesdays are protected focus time; no internal meetings should be scheduled
- All recurring meetings must have an agenda shared 24 hours in advance or they can be declined without explanation
- Meetings default to 25 minutes (not 30) and 50 minutes (not 60) to allow transition time
- Video on is the norm for meetings with 5 or fewer people; optional for large all-hands

### Documentation
- Decisions are written down in Notion; "if it's not in Notion, it didn't happen"
- Each team maintains a team homepage in Notion with: OKRs, ongoing projects, team roster, runbooks
- Engineering additionally documents in GitHub (ADRs — Architecture Decision Records — for significant technical decisions)

### All-Hands
- **Monthly company all-hands:** First Friday of every month, 11 AM IST, 90 minutes. Recorded for async viewing.
- **Quarterly business review (QBR):** Full-day event for all managers and above; includes financial results, product roadmap, and competitive landscape
- **Annual offsite:** 2 days in November, Bengaluru. Entire company invited (travel and accommodation covered). Mix of strategy, team building, and recognition.

## OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)

Elanco runs OKRs on a quarterly basis at the company, department, and team level.

### Cadence
- **Planning:** Last 2 weeks of each quarter (Q-end) for next quarter's OKRs
- **Check-in:** Mid-quarter (Week 6–7) — async progress update in Notion
- **Grading:** Last week of the quarter; 0.0–1.0 score per KR
- Target score: 0.7 (intentionally ambitious; 1.0 every quarter means targets were too easy)

### Current Company OKRs (Q4 FY2024–25, Jan–Mar 2025)
**Objective 1:** Strengthen Companion Animal market leadership in South India
- KR1: Achieve 115% of Flexi-Guard sales target in Karnataka and Tamil Nadu combined
- KR2: Onboard 400 new veterinary clinics in Tier 2 cities (below 10 lakh population)
- KR3: Launch NeuroCalm in 500 clinics outside metro areas

**Objective 2:** Accelerate technology platform reliability
- KR1: Reduce VetConnect Portal p95 API latency from 420 ms to under 200 ms
- KR2: Achieve 99.9% uptime for all Tier 1 services (vs. 99.6% in Q3)
- KR3: Complete SOC 2 Type II evidence collection

**Objective 3:** Build people and culture for scale
- KR1: Reduce voluntary attrition from 14% to under 11% (annualised)
- KR2: Achieve 80%+ participation in annual engagement survey (currently 71%)
- KR3: Promote 15 engineers from L2 to L3 or above

## Recognition

### Elanco Stars Programme
Monthly peer-nominated recognition. Anyone can nominate a colleague via the ElancoStars Slack channel using the `/star` command. Nominations are reviewed by department heads; 3–5 winners announced at the monthly all-hands.

**Award:** ₹5,000 gift voucher (Amazon or Swiggy) + feature in the internal newsletter

### Founder's Award
Annual. Nominated by the CEO and senior leadership team. Recognises one individual or team that exemplified "above and beyond" contribution to the mission.

**Award:** ₹1,00,000 cash + a week off + feature in the annual report

### Long Service Recognition
- 3 years: personalised Elanco-branded gift set
- 5 years: ₹25,000 bonus + 2 days of additional leave
- 10 years: ₹75,000 bonus + 5 days of additional leave + CEO lunch

## Diversity, Equity & Inclusion

Elanco's DEI programme is led by **Deepa Nair (CHRO)** and governed by a DEI Council that meets monthly.

### Current Numbers (FY2024)
- Women in leadership (Director and above): 47%
- Women in engineering: 38% (vs. 20% industry average)
- Employees from Tier 2 / Tier 3 cities: 41%
- Employees with disabilities: 3.2% (target: 5% by 2026)

### Active Programmes
- **ElancoPride:** LGBTQ+ inclusion; gender-neutral bathroom policy across all offices; partner benefits extended to same-sex couples
- **Return-to-Work:** Programme for professionals returning after a career break of 2+ years; 6-month supported re-entry with a buddy and adjusted ramp-up expectations
- **Rural Talent Pipeline:** Partnerships with engineering colleges in Tier 2 cities (Tier 2 cities: Mysuru, Mangaluru, Coimbatore, Nashik, Nagpur); dedicated internship programme
- **Inclusive Hiring:** Blind resume screening for all roles below L4; structured interviews with consistent rubrics
