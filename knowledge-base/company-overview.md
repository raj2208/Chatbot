# Elanco — Company Overview

## Who We Are

Elanco is a global animal health company headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with regional offices in Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Singapore. Founded in 2011 by Dr. Priya Menon and Arjun Dasgupta, Elanco develops, manufactures, and markets pharmaceutical products and vaccines for companion animals (pets) and livestock.

As of 2024, Elanco employs approximately 3,200 people across 18 countries. The company is privately held and reported revenues of ₹4,800 crore (approximately $580 million USD) in FY2024.

## Mission and Vision

**Mission:** To improve animal health and, through it, enrich the lives of people who depend on animals — as companions, as food sources, and as working partners.

**Vision:** A world where every animal gets the care it deserves.

## Core Values

- **Science First** — Every product decision is grounded in peer-reviewed evidence and clinical trials.
- **Animal Welfare** — We do not compromise on humane treatment in any of our research or manufacturing processes.
- **Integrity** — We operate transparently with regulators, customers, and each other.
- **Innovation** — We invest 12% of annual revenue into R&D, above the industry average of 8%.
- **Inclusion** — 47% of our leadership team are women; 38% come from non-metro India.

## Company Timeline

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 2011 | Founded in Bengaluru by Dr. Priya Menon (CSO) and Arjun Dasgupta (CEO) |
| 2013 | Launched Flexi-Guard, our first flea and tick vaccine for dogs |
| 2015 | Opened manufacturing plant in Pune; received FSSAI certification |
| 2017 | Expanded into livestock with AgroShield product line |
| 2019 | Series C funding round of $120 million led by OrbisMed |
| 2021 | Acquired VetCare Analytics, a veterinary data platform |
| 2022 | Opened Singapore regional office; entered Southeast Asian markets |
| 2023 | Launched NeuroCalm, our first behavioural health product for pets |
| 2024 | Crossed ₹4,800 crore revenue; began clinical trials for Bovine Mastitis vaccine |
