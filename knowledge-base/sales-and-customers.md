## Customer Segments

### Companion Animal

**Veterinary Clinics**
The primary channel. Elanco has relationships with 14,000 registered veterinary clinics across India. Clinics receive:
- Preferred pricing (15–22% below MRP depending on volume tier)
- Monthly credit terms (net-30)
- Clinical education materials and product samples
- Access to VetConnect Portal for ordering and clinical resources

Key clinic clusters: Bengaluru (1,800), Mumbai (1,600), Delhi NCR (1,400), Hyderabad (1,100), Chennai (900)

**Pet Specialty Retail**
- Partnered with: PetZone (320 stores), Heads Up For Tails (210 stores), Pet Central (150 stores)
- Products: DentalGuard Chews, VetShield Dewormer, grooming add-ons
- Retail margin: 28–32%
- Display placement fee (slotting) negotiated annually

**E-commerce**
- Direct-to-consumer via elanco.in/shop
- Also listed on: Amazon, Flipkart, Supertails, Heads Up For Tails online
- Online channel grew 64% YoY in FY2024; now 12% of companion animal revenue
- Prescription products (NeuroCalm, Flexi-Guard) require vet prescription upload before purchase

**Shelter Partnerships**
- 40+ animal shelters receive Flexi-Guard and VetShield at cost price (CSR programme)
- Managed by the Marketing team, not Sales

---

### Livestock & Agriculture

**Dairy Cooperatives**
- Major accounts: Amul (Gujarat), Heritage Dairy (Andhra Pradesh/Telangana), Nandini (Karnataka)
- Products: AgroShield Mastitis Control, AgroShield FMD Vaccine
- Contract structure: annual supply agreements with quarterly delivery schedules
- These three cooperatives represent 34% of Livestock division revenue

**Poultry Integrators**
- Key accounts: Suguna Foods, Srinivasa Farms, Venkateshwara Hatcheries
- Product: AgroShield GrowMax (feed additive)
- Sold in bulk 25 kg bags; distributed via agri-distributors

**Government Tenders**
- AgroShield FMD Vaccine is supplied to state animal husbandry departments under government livestock health schemes
- States active: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, West Bengal, Odisha
- Government business represents 28% of Livestock revenue; lower margin but high volume and brand credibility

**Agri-Distributors**
- Network of 380 distributors covering rural India
- Distributors stock the full AgroShield range and sell to individual farmers and small farms
- Monthly sales meets for top 50 distributors; quarterly for the rest

## Marketing

### Digital Marketing
- Channels: Instagram (340K followers), YouTube (180K subscribers), LinkedIn (95K followers)
- Primary content format: educational videos for pet owners ("Is your dog anxious? Here's why")
- SEO: elanco.in ranks #1 for "flea tick medicine for dogs India" and "dog dewormer India"
- Paid: Google Ads and Meta Ads; ₹8 crore annual digital marketing spend

### Veterinary Education (VetEd Programme)
- Monthly webinars for registered veterinarians; ~2,000 attendees per session
- Annual "Elanco Vet Summit" in Bengaluru; 800+ vets attended in 2024
- Continuing education credits (CECs) provided; recognised by Veterinary Council of India

### Trade Marketing
- Co-branded clinic displays for top 500 clinics
- Flexi-Guard refrigerator units placed in 1,200 clinics (product requires cold storage)
- Annual "Elanco Clinic of the Year" award — cash prize of ₹2 lakh + trophy
