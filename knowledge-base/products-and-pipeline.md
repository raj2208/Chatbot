# Elanco — Products and R&D Pipeline

## Product Categories

Elanco's portfolio is divided into two business units: **Companion Animal Health** and **Livestock & Agriculture Health**.

---

## Companion Animal Health

### Flexi-Guard (2013)
- **Type:** Flea, tick, and mite preventative — topical spot-on
- **Species:** Dogs and cats
- **Mechanism:** Inhibits insect nerve function via selective sodium channel blocking; does not affect mammalian channels
- **Dosage:** Monthly application; available in 4 weight-based SKUs (< 5 kg, 5–10 kg, 10–25 kg, > 25 kg)
- **Markets:** India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Singapore, Malaysia
- **Annual units sold (FY2024):** 8.2 million doses
- **Key differentiator:** 98.4% efficacy in field trials vs. 91% industry average

### NeuroCalm (2023)
- **Type:** Behavioural health — oral tablet
- **Species:** Dogs
- **Indication:** Anxiety disorders, noise phobia (Diwali/fireworks anxiety), separation anxiety
- **Active ingredient:** Zylpirofen (Elanco proprietary compound)
- **Dosage:** Once daily; available in 10 mg and 25 mg tablets
- **Markets:** India only (pending regulatory approval in Singapore and Malaysia)
- **Status:** Fastest-selling Elanco launch; ₹180 crore revenue in first year
- **Ongoing study:** 12-month longitudinal safety study in 2,400 dogs across 6 Indian cities

### VetShield Dewormer
- **Type:** Broad-spectrum anthelmintic (dewormer)
- **Species:** Dogs, cats, cattle
- **Active ingredients:** Albendazole + Praziquantel combination
- **Dosage:** Single oral dose; body-weight-based
- **Markets:** All Elanco markets
- **Note:** Highest volume product by units; sold primarily through veterinary clinics and rural veterinary dispensaries

### DentalGuard Chews
- **Type:** Dental hygiene supplement — functional chew
- **Species:** Dogs
- **Claim:** Reduces plaque by 62% and tartar by 48% over 30 days (clinical trial data)
- **Variants:** Chicken, beef, and vegetarian (soy-based)
- **Distribution:** Veterinary clinics, pet specialty retail, and Elanco's own e-commerce portal

## Livestock & Agriculture Health (AgroShield Line)

### AgroShield FMD Vaccine
- **Type:** Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD) vaccine — injectable
- **Species:** Cattle, buffalo, goats, pigs
- **Coverage:** Serotypes O, A, and Asia-1 (trivalent formulation)
- **Dosage:** 2 ml IM; two doses 4 weeks apart, annual booster
- **Cold chain:** 2–8°C; partnered with GoFrigo for cold-chain logistics in rural India
- **Markets:** India (17 states), Nepal, Bangladesh
- **Scale:** Supplied to 3 state government livestock vaccination programmes

### AgroShield Mastitis Control
- **Type:** Intramammary antibiotic — lactating cow preparation
- **Active ingredient:** Cefoperazone sodium 500 mg per syringe
- **Indication:** Treatment and prevention of bovine mastitis (leading cause of dairy farm losses)
- **Distribution:** Direct to dairy cooperatives (Amul, Heritage, Nandini partnerships)

### AgroShield GrowMax (Growth Promoter)
- **Type:** Nutritional supplement — feed additive
- **Species:** Broiler poultry and swine
- **Claim:** 11% improvement in feed conversion ratio (FCR) in controlled trials
- **Regulatory status:** Approved under FSSAI feed additive schedule; antibiotic-free formulation
