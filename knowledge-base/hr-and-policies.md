# Elanco — HR Policies & People Programmes

## Employment Structure

Elanco employs three categories of workers:

| Type | Count (2024) | Benefits Eligible |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Full-time permanent | 2,640 | Full |
| Fixed-term contract (FTC) | 380 | Partial (no ESOP) |
| Consultants / freelancers | 180 | None |

All full-time offers are made in Indian Rupees (INR) for India-based roles and in SGD for Singapore roles.

## Leave Policy

### Annual Leave
- **0–2 years tenure:** 18 days per year
- **2–5 years tenure:** 21 days per year
- **5+ years tenure:** 24 days per year
- Leave accrues monthly (1.5–2 days/month depending on tier)
- Up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next calendar year; remainder lapses
- Leave encashment is allowed at exit for up to 30 accumulated days

### Sick Leave
- 12 days per calendar year, non-encashable
- Medical certificate required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days
- Chronic illness cases are handled under a separate Long-Term Illness Support policy (consult HR directly)

### Public Holidays
- 10 national/state holidays fixed by HQ each January
- 3 floating holidays that employees can use for personal observances
- Field sales staff have a separate roster due to customer-facing requirements

### Parental Leave
- **Primary caregiver (birthing parent):** 26 weeks fully paid
- **Secondary caregiver / adoption (primary):** 26 weeks fully paid
- **Secondary caregiver (non-birthing partner):** 4 weeks fully paid
- Phased return-to-work: optional 60% workload for 4 weeks post-leave with full pay
- Fertility treatment leave: up to 10 days per cycle, up to 3 cycles per year (confidential)

### Bereavement Leave
- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 days
- Extended family (grandparent, in-law): 3 days
- Miscarriage / pregnancy loss: 10 days (primary) / 5 days (partner)

### Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
- Applicable when working on a declared holiday
- Must be claimed within 60 days; no cash equivalent

## Compensation & Benefits

### Salary Structure
Elanco uses a **70/30 pay structure** for most roles:
- 70% fixed (base salary)
- 30% variable (annual performance bonus)

For senior leadership (VP and above), this shifts to 60/40.

### Performance Bonus
- Paid annually in April (for the financial year ending March 31)
- Based on: company performance (50%), business unit performance (25%), individual rating (25%)
- Rating scale: Exceeds (1.3x multiplier), Meets (1.0x), Partially Meets (0.5x), Does Not Meet (0%)
- FY2024 payout was at 108% of target due to revenue outperformance

### ESOP (Employee Stock Option Plan)
- Available to all permanent employees after 6 months
- Vesting schedule: 25% at end of Year 1, then 6.25% every quarter for Years 2–4 (4-year total vest)
- Strike price set at FMV at grant date; grants reviewed annually each July
- Pool size: 8% of total equity reserved for employees

### Provident Fund & Gratuity
- PF: 12% employer contribution on basic salary (statutory)
- Gratuity: Applicable after 5 years of service; calculated per Payment of Gratuity Act 1972

### Health Insurance
- Mediclaim cover: ₹5 lakh per annum (employee + spouse + up to 2 children)
- Top-up available: additional ₹10 lakh at subsidised group premium (employee-paid)
- Parents can be added at employee cost
- Dental and vision: ₹15,000 per year reimbursement claim
- Mental health: ₹20,000 per year for therapy sessions (no approval required; direct reimbursement)
- Accidental death & disability cover: 3× annual CTC

### Other Benefits
- Internet reimbursement: ₹1,500/month for permanently remote employees
- Meal allowance: ₹3,200/month for office-based employees
- Learning & development budget: ₹50,000 per employee per year (courses, certifications, conferences)
- Gym / wellness reimbursement: ₹10,000 per year

## Work Arrangements

### Office-First Policy (Bengaluru HQ and Pune Plant)
- Expected in-office: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Monday and Friday: flexible (work from home allowed)
- Manufacturing and lab roles: 5 days on-site (non-negotiable due to safety requirements)

### Fully Remote Roles
- Approved for: field sales, certain Data & AI engineers, and roles hired outside Elanco office cities
- Remote employees must be reachable during core hours: **10:00 AM – 5:00 PM IST**
- Annual in-person gathering (all hands): 2 days in Bengaluru each November (travel and accommodation covered)

### International Travel
- Business travel booked through the Elanco travel portal (TravelDesk, powered by FCM Travel)
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; business class for 6+ hours
- Daily allowance (per diem) for international travel: $75 USD/day (Singapore), $60 USD/day (other Asia)

## Performance Management

### Review Cycle
- **Mid-year check-in:** September (no rating; development-focused conversation)
- **Annual review:** February–March
- Ratings are calibrated in department-level calibration sessions before being communicated

### PIP (Performance Improvement Plan)
- Triggered when an employee receives "Does Not Meet" in two consecutive quarters
- Duration: 60–90 days with specific, measurable milestones
- HR Business Partner must be involved from day one

### Promotion Cycle
- Promotions are effective April 1 (aligned with financial year start)
- Nominations submitted by managers in January; approved by VP and CHRO by March
- Engineers follow a defined levelling framework: L1 (Junior) → L2 → L3 → L4 (Senior) → L5 (Staff) → L6 (Principal)

## Learning & Development

- **ElancoLearn:** Internal LMS with 400+ courses (technical, compliance, leadership)
- **External budget:** ₹50,000/year per employee for external courses, certifications, or conferences
- **Manager development programme:** 6-month cohort for first-time managers; runs twice a year
- **Leadership accelerator:** For L4+ engineers and senior individual contributors identified as future leaders
- **GCP certification sponsorship:** 100% cost covered; study time (up to 8 hours/sprint) is supported

## Code of Conduct Highlights

- Gifts from vendors: permitted up to ₹2,000 in value; anything above must be declared to compliance team
- Conflict of interest: must be disclosed annually via the online COI declaration form
- Whistleblower policy: anonymous reporting via EthicsPoint portal; zero retaliation policy
- Data privacy: all employees must complete DPDP Act training annually (mandatory)
- Social media: employees may not disclose unreleased product information or financial data publicly
